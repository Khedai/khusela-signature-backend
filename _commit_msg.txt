Send the signing link from the app's own mail client instead of from this service: POST /api/notify/email and mailer.js's sendSigningLink are gone, and the PWA's "Send via Email" is now a mailto: that opens the consultant's mail app with the link written and addressed to the client.

The reason is the host, not the code: the free Render tier this service runs on blocks outbound traffic to SMTP ports 25, 465 and 587 (Render changelog, 26 September 2025), so no mailbox configured on this service can be reached from it. A connection to smtp-relay.brevo.com:587 timed out with credentials that send perfectly from anywhere else, so there was nothing to fix in the transport, the key or the port. A redirect needs no mailbox, no SMTP port and no provider, and it leaves the message in the consultant's own sent folder, which is where "the client says they never got it" can be answered. WhatsApp was already a redirect and needed nothing; email now matches it.

The route's recipient-from-the-database-row design, its token-hash check and its suite are removed with it rather than left behind as dead code, and README, .env.example, render.yaml and npm test are back to describing only the application email.
